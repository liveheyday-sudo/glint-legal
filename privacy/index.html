Glint Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

DonaeLabs (“Glint”, “we”, “us”) makes the Glint app, a nutrition and wellness companion for adults using GLP-1 medications. Glint is available in the United States. This policy explains what stays on your device, the limited data that leaves it and why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

Not medical advice. Glint provides general nutrition and wellness information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace care from a qualified clinician.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored in the app’s database on your device and is not backed up to Glint’s servers:

  • Your logs: meals and nutrition, water, weight entries, injections and oral doses, mood, and side effects.
  • Your chat history with the in-app coach.
  • Your progress photos.
  • Your profile health details (height, age, biological sex, food aversions and allergies).
  • Your saved meals.
  • Your medication details (medication, dose, and shot-day schedule). Editing them in Glint updates your device only.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Local Data. It is also removed when you delete the app or delete your account in the app. Your device may include app data in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup) under Apple’s terms; Glint does not control those backups.

2. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Glint asks to read your body weight only. Glint does not write to Apple Health and does not read other Health data.

  • Weight read from Apple Health is stored on your device and used on your device for your weight and progress features.
  • Weight read from Apple Health is not sent to Glint’s servers and not sent to AI providers. AI features use only weight you entered in Glint yourself (Section 4).
  • We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it or share it with data brokers.
  • If you export your data or share a Doctor Report, you choose where the file goes; it can include weight from Apple Health. Glint does not send these files anywhere itself.
  • Disconnecting Apple Health in Glint (Settings → Apple Health → Disconnect from Glint) stops new imports. Weights already imported stay on your device until you delete your local data or your account. You can also revoke access in the iOS Health app or Settings.

4. AI features

Glint’s AI features use third-party AI providers. Before you first use AI chat or AI photo scanning, Glint shows you what is shared and asks you to agree; you can use Glint without these features. Requests pass through Glint’s servers, which authenticate you, apply limits, and route the request. Your name, email, and account ID are not included in AI request content.

  • Food and nutrition-label photos: the photo you choose is sent to Google (Gemini), with OpenAI as a backup, to identify foods and estimate nutrition.
  • AI coach and meal descriptions: your message and your recent conversation (up to your last 20 messages) are sent to OpenAI or Anthropic with limited context: today’s nutrition and targets; your goal weight and weight you entered in Glint (latest weight and total lost); your logging streak; and medication timing (a generic “GLP-1 medication” label, week on the current dose, injection day and time, and days since your last injection). Your medication brand and dose are not included.
  • Symptom questions: if your message is about a symptom or side effect, your side effects from the last 7 days (names and severity) are included.
  • Optional personalization: if you turn on profile-based AI personalization, your age, sex, and height are included.
  • Progress summaries and patterns: when you ask how your week went, a summary of the past 7 days (nutrition averages, goal days, side-effect counts, and weights you entered) is sent to Anthropic. Glint may then send up to 30 days of your logs (meals, weights you entered, injection dates, side effects, and water) to Anthropic to look for patterns.
  • Never sent to AI providers: weight from Apple Health; your name, email, or account ID; your medication brand or dose; your progress photos.

AI providers process request content to return results and may retain it for a limited time under their own terms and policies.

5. Food search and barcodes

Glint looks up common foods on your device first. For other searches and barcode scans, your search terms and barcode numbers are sent directly from your device to Open Food Facts to find nutrition data. No account information is included; like any internet request, the service can see your device’s IP address.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data (email, user ID, goals, subscription status, push token): while your account exists; deleted when you delete your account.
  • AI request content: not stored by Glint after routing; AI providers may retain it under their own policies.
  • Service usage records: automatically deleted after 400 days.
  • Notification delivery records: automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Crash reports: kept by our crash-reporting provider under its retention settings.
  • On-device data: until you delete it, delete your account, or delete the app.

8. Deleting your account and data

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and the data linked to it on our servers (email, goals, subscription status, push tokens, service usage records, and related records), then clears Glint’s data on that device.
  • What remains: an App Store transaction identifier, no longer linked to you, kept to prevent subscription fraud; AI request content and crash reports already held by those providers under their policies; Apple’s own purchase records; and device backups you control.
  • Delete on-device data only: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Local Data.

9. Eligibility

Glint is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Security

Account credentials are stored in your device’s secure keystore (iOS Keychain), and data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Your choices

You control Apple Health access, AI features, optional AI personalization, and notifications, and you can delete your local data or your account at any time. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact us (Section 13).
